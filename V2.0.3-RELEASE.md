# V2.0.3 — property-aware free cash flow

The old statement omitted property cash costs even though the property lifecycle correctly posted them to cash and net worth. FCF now uses compensation plus posted cash interest and net rental distributions, less taxes, non-housing household spending, actual generic housing, primary-home cash costs, non-property debt service, and purchase closing/setup and selling costs. Generic housing is replaced, not added twice. Rental distributions already include vacancy, maintenance, capex, management, taxes, insurance, HOA and debt service; those costs are not deducted again. NOI continues to exclude financing.

Scheduled principal is cash debt service but remains a net-worth transfer. Down payments, investments, employee retirement contributions, household/property reserve funding, released reserves and sale proceeds/payoffs are capital transfers, not consumption or income. Employer match remains an external retirement-account inflow, not spendable household FCF. The existing property model has reserve funding and release but no separate reserve-paid-expense event; modeled maintenance/capex is expensed when posted, not when reserves are funded. No new reserve-spending engine was introduced.

Zero versus $1,000 primary maintenance now changes cash, FCF, property operations, closing account activity and net worth by exactly $1,000. The decomposition's primary carrying-cost component changes once; debt and reserve funding do not double-count it. Existing integrated account activity retains its aggregate property adjustment fields; its closing amount agrees with the cash ledger. The cash statement no longer labels investment contributions as an FCF deduction or presents mixed gross/net property components as an additive equation.

Five focused regressions cover the maintenance trace, generic-housing replacement, net rental treatment, transaction expenses versus capital transfers, and cash interest. The former FCF test now asserts equality to the operating ledger. Approved golden fingerprints change because FCF and dependent goal-FCF explanations change. A before/after integrated-fixture audit confirmed identical accounts, balance sheets, properties, taxes, compensation and household spending in every year; purchase/execution milestone tests remain unchanged.

Version: engine v2.0.3. Output shape/contract stays 2.0.2; production model/schema stay 2.0.0. No migration. Full suite: 320 tests pass. V1 root remains unchanged.
