# V2.1.2 — Life Plan setup and exploration

Life Plan is now the recommended first-run option alongside Scratch, temporary Example and Import. It supplies editable education, JAG, optional pediatrics, spending, reserve, investment and long-term-home structure without inventing balances, debt, BAH, duty stations or transfer dates.

Minimum setup: birth year, starting filing status, a user-entered BAH estimate in 2026 dollars, and explicit account balances (including an explicit remaining-balances-to-zero action). Partner is opt-in with a visible same-age assumption; detailed setup remains available. Creation is unsaved, replacement requires confirmation, and a successful save opens Overview.

Overview now includes a dominant full-width wealth chart, planned life milestones and authoritative goal/property milestones, selected-year income and asset composition, and a model-based plan summary. Education is shown as the selected chapter. Goal timing distinguishes first possible, first comfortable and execution; the Timeline defaults to goal status changes and retains all annual evaluations under its Goal filter. Age changes reuse the completed simulation.

## Modeling limits to review

- The budget is $4,550/month in 2025 dollars throughout the horizon; it is not a researched college cost estimate. Education's $250/month is provisional. Actual school funding, work, family support and education debt need customization. An explicit-zero example therefore shows unfunded school-year spending.
- JAG starts at planned age 25 and ends after 24 planned service years. Future O-2/O-3 events are editable assumptions. Automatic service-based pension benefits, retirement withdrawals, Social Security retirement and state income taxes are not implemented. Later-life balances are not a retirement adequacy forecast.
- Partner is optional; residency and attending stages are separate. Same-age timing is a disclosed default, not a personal fact. Household combination does not change marital or tax status.
- Expected property transfer is $200,000 on the existing nominal-at-transfer basis, undated and excluded from wealth. No actual home, rental purchase or PCS is invented.
- The home target is $1.5M in 2025 purchasing power. Editable costs include 6% financing, 20% down, 2% closing, 1.8% property tax, 0.5% insurance, 1% maintenance, $20,000 setup and $20,000 initial reserve. These are planning defaults, not quotes.
- Planning Outlook and the vehicle engine are deferred. V1 stays at `/`; V2 stays at `/v2/`.
