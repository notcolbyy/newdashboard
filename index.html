Rules designed to prevent overextending

Positive cash flow

A rental should cover mortgage, taxes, insurance, HOA, vacancy, maintenance, management, and still leave a surplus.

Six-month reserves

Keep six months of modeled operating costs per rental before adding another property.

Primary-residence intent

Only model VA financing when the property is genuinely intended to be occupied as a primary home.

Lifestyle grows later

Keep early fixed costs reasonable, then add nicer cars, travel, and discretionary spending as income and assets grow.
